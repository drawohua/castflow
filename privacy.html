Castflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Castflow is a Mac app that plays your video files on a Chromecast or AirPlay device on your own network. It is designed to work without collecting any information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Castflow has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services. The developer receives no data from the app.

Your videos and subtitles

Videos and subtitle files you open are read on your Mac. To play them on your TV, Castflow streams them directly from your Mac to the device you choose, over your local network. They are never uploaded to the internet or to the developer. Temporary video segments made during playback are kept in the app's private storage on your Mac and deleted as playback moves on and when you stop.

Local network

Castflow asks for Local Network access so it can find Chromecast devices and send video to them. It only talks to devices on your network that you choose to cast to.

Information stored on your Mac

To be convenient, Castflow remembers a few things on your Mac only: where you stopped in each video, the TV you used last, your subtitle settings, and folders you allowed it to read subtitles from. You can remove all of this by deleting the app.

Third parties

When you cast, your Chromecast or Apple TV plays the stream. Those devices are made by Google and Apple and are covered by their own privacy policies. Castflow does not send them anything except the video you chose to play.

Children

Castflow does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
