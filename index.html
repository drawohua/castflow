Castflow

Play the videos on your Mac on your TV.

Castflow streams video files from your Mac to Chromecast and Google TV devices, and to Apple TV with AirPlay.

  • Plays MKV, MP4, AVI and more, converting on the fly when the TV needs it
  • Subtitles from the video or from files next to it, with size and timing adjustments
  • Remembers where you stopped and plays the next episode automatically
  • Native for Apple silicon

Coming to the Mac App Store.
